**open-homm5 0.2.3** adds a player list on the map, showing who is still playing and who has ended their turn, and a notification when your turn begins while you are in another window. It is one file, `dinput8.dll`, plus a settings file and 24 ready random map templates; the game's own files are never changed. You need the game with **MMH5.5 already installed**.

## New in 0.2.3

- **Player list on the adventure map.** A column on the left edge, in the game's own style:
  - **The day,** and how many humans have ended their turn: "Jour 34 - 3/4 Prêts", or English when the game is in English.
  - **Teams:** each has a numbered button.
  - **Each player:** their current hero's portrait in their colour, their name, and a status mark:
    - green dot: playing;
    - tick: turn ended;
    - blinking dots: the computer is thinking;
    - empty ring: waiting.
  - **F9** hides or shows it. It fades when the mouse is over it.
  - **Size:** it grows with the screen like the game's own interface, and stays clear of the chat box and the minimap.
  - **Settings** under `[ui]`: `player_list=0` turns it off; `player_list_key`, `player_list_top`, `player_list_left` and `language=auto|en|fr` adjust it.
- **"Your turn" notification.** When your turn begins and the game is not the window in front:
  - the game's taskbar button flashes until you come back;
  - a short sound plays: Windows' notification sound, or a small chime on Linux;
  - a pop-up says "Your turn (day N)" (Windows).

  Nothing happens on the first turn of a game or when you are already looking at the game. Settings under `[notify]`: `turn=0` turns it off; `sound=0` and `popup=0` turn off the sound or the pop-up.

Both only read the game, so they can't cause a desync.

## Also in this version (from 0.2.0 to 0.2.2)

- **No more long autosave freeze** at the start of each round. It went from 7 to 10 s down to about 0.2 s when the `Saves` folder holds many multiplayer saves.
- **Quicker multiplayer.** When every player has the add-on, the PCs agree on 50 ms game steps, show results sooner, and pace themselves better.
- **Teams on random maps**, and on any map where every player starts alone. The host picks the teams with the lobby's arrows (`lobby_teams=1` under `[rmg]`) or a setting: `teams=humans_vs_ai`, `pairs`, or a list like `1,1,2,2`.
- **24 ready random map templates in the game's list** (2v2, 2v2v2, 3v3, 4v4, free-for-all). **Every player needs the same templates.**

## Install

1. Download `open-homm5-0.2.3.zip` below and extract it.
2. Install:
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`.
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options.
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install this version.**

The installer keeps your `open-homm5.ini`. New settings work without being in it.

## Check that it works

- The first line of `open-homm5.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `open-homm5 hook DLL 0.2.3 loaded`.
- In a co-op game, the log says `co-op timing: ON`.
- On the adventure map, the player list appears on the left.

## Good to know

- **Not tested yet:** the player list in fullscreen mode and on Linux, and the notification on Linux. If something looks wrong, send your `open-homm5.log`.
- **Uninstall:** `uninstall.bat` (Windows) or `bash uninstall.sh` (Linux).
